# BUG-004: Unable to Remove Products from Cart

**Severity:** High  
**Priority:** High  
**Status:** Open  
**Module:** Shopping Cart  
**Environment:** Chromium browser  
**Application:** Toolshop – Bug-injected build  
**URL:** https://with-bugs.practicesoftwaretesting.com/#/checkout

## Preconditions
- The website is accessible.
- At least one product has been added to the cart.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the bug-injected website.
2. Open a product and add it to the cart.
3. Open the shopping cart.
4. Click the red Remove (X) control beside the product.
5. Check whether the product disappears.

## Expected Result
The product should be removed. If it was the only product, an empty-cart message should appear.

## Actual Result
The product remains in the cart after clicking Remove.

## Evidence
- Screenshot showing the product still present after the removal attempt.
- Automated test: `tests/test_cart.py::test_remove_product_from_cart`

## Impact
Customers cannot remove unwanted products from their carts, potentially leading to incorrect orders.

## Reference Build Comparison
- Reference build: Removal test passed.
- Bug-injected build: Removal test failed.

## Reproducibility
Reproduced manually and through the automated Playwright test.
